OOP PRINCIPLE 1

🔒 Encapsulation

Defensive private variables and invariant enforcement in Product, Customer, and Order. Prevents negative stock and invalid prices.

OOP PRINCIPLE 2

🧬 Inheritance

Abstract class DiscountStrategyBase extended by concrete coupon subclasses: PercentageDiscount, FlatDiscount, and BankOfferDiscount.

OOP PRINCIPLE 3

🎭 Polymorphism

Strategy Pattern through interface DiscountStrategy. Common calling service invokes calculateDiscount() uniformly across algorithms.

OOP PRINCIPLE 4

⚠️ Custom Exceptions

User-defined checked exception OutOfStockException communicating product ID, requested units, and remaining stock without crashing threads.

OOP PRINCIPLE 5

⚡ Multithreading & Synchronization

CustomerPurchaseTask implements Runnable, CountDownLatch synchronization barrier, and atomic synchronized (inventoryLock) monitors.

OOP PRINCIPLE 6

💾 Collections & Persistence

LinkedHashMap for O(1) lookups, ArrayList, explicit Iterator<Order> traversal, and thread-safe UTF-8 CSV stream persistence.
